ALGEBRA · Q1
Factorize the expression completely:
2x² + 6x - 20
Step 1: Factor out 2 → 2(x² + 3x − 10)
Step 2: Factorize → 2(x + 5)(x − 2)
Answer: 2(x + 5)(x − 2)
LINEAR EQUATIONS · Q2
Solve simultaneously:
y = 2x + 1    y = x + 4
Step 1: Set equal → 2x + 1 = x + 4
Step 2: Solve → x = 3
Step 3: Substitute → y = 7
Answer: x = 3, y = 7
GEOMETRY · Q3
Find the hypotenuse if the two sides are 6cm and 8cm:
a² + b² = c²
Step 1: 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100
Step 2: c = √100
Answer: c = 10 cm
FUNCTIONS · Q4
Find the x-intercepts of the function:
f(x) = x² − 5x + 6
Step 1: Factorize → (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0
Step 2: Solve → x = 2 or x = 3
Answer: x-intercepts at (2, 0) and (3, 0)
